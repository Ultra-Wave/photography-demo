About the Studio

We See the
World Differently

Hi, I'm Sophia Laurent — photographer, light-chaser, and the founder of Lumière Studio. I've spent over a decade perfecting the art of documentary-style photography that feels as honest as it does beautiful.

Based in New York but shooting worldwide, I believe the best images happen when your photographer disappears into the room — so you forget the camera is even there. That's when real magic occurs.

My work has been featured in Vogue Weddings, Harper's Bazaar, The Times Magazine, and recognized with multiple international awards.
